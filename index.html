Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about buccal fat removal and your consultation.

What is buccal fat removal?

Buccal fat removal is a facial contouring procedure that removes part of the buccal fat pad, the pocket of fat in the lower cheek. It reduces fullness and gives the midface a slimmer, more sculpted look.

Who is a good candidate for buccal fat pad removal?

People who feel their lower cheeks look full or round, even at a stable weight, are often good candidates. Your face shape, skin quality and age all matter, so a personal consultation is the only way to know if it suits you.

How long is the recovery?

Many people return to normal routines within about a week, with some swelling that settles over the following weeks. Your surgeon will give you specific aftercare instructions, and healing varies from person to person.

Will my face look too thin or hollow?

Not when the procedure is planned carefully. We take a conservative approach and look at your overall facial balance and how your face may change with age, so the result stays natural.
